Undo the AI in a document

A generated document gives itself away in its characters long before its arguments — curly quotes, em dashes, non-breaking spaces, zero-width marks nobody can see, and a set of document properties naming the tool that wrote it. Drop a .docx or .pptx here and get back the same document with all of that removed, plus a list of what came out.

upload

Word (.docx) and PowerPoint (.pptx). Several at a time is fine. Nothing is uploaded — the file is opened, rewritten and handed back inside this tab.

what it rewrites

  • Curly quotes and apostrophes, em and en dashes, ellipsis characters, primes.
  • Non-breaking and hair spaces, zero-width joiners, bidi controls, and the Unicode tag characters some generators leave behind as an invisible watermark.
  • Bullet glyphs, arrows, typographic maths symbols, emoji.
  • Literal markdown (**bold**, ### heading) and chat citation markers.
  • Assistant asides — “Certainly! Here’s your report.”, “I hope this helps!”
  • Author, last-edited-by, timestamps, revision count, generator tags, custom properties, thumbnails, editing-session IDs, comments, tracked changes, hidden text.
  • Links to AI tools, and the utm_source=chatgpt.com tail on the rest.

what it won’t do

  • It doesn’t rewrite your sentences. Stock phrasing is counted and listed; changing it is a judgement call, and a find-and-replace would make the writing worse.
  • It doesn’t make text undetectable, and nothing can. Detectors read style and structure, not punctuation — this removes the mechanical traces, not the fact that a model helped.
  • It doesn’t touch your layout, images, fonts or styles. Media is copied through byte for byte.
  • Old binary .doc and .ppt files aren’t supported — re-save them first.
